Delete Your BlackTest Account

App: BlackTest  |  Developer: BlackTest

You can ask us to permanently delete your BlackTest account and the data linked to it at any time. You do not need to have the app installed to make this request.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email from the email address registered to your BlackTest account to support@newtowns.in.
  2. Use the subject line "Delete my BlackTest account".
  3. Include your registered name and email address in the message.
  4. We may reply to verify that you own the account. Please respond so we can proceed.
  5. Once verified, we will delete your account and data and send you a confirmation email.
Email a deletion request
Timeline: We acknowledge requests within 72 hours and complete deletion within 30 days of verification.

What gets deleted

DataAction
Account credentials (email, password)Permanently deleted
Profile information (name, phone number)Permanently deleted
Uploaded PDF files and document recordsPermanently deleted
Extracted questions and practice sets created from your PDFsPermanently deleted
Test attempts, scores and performance historyPermanently deleted
Extraction job records and search historyPermanently deleted

What may be kept

  • Information we are legally required to retain (for example, to comply with applicable law or resolve disputes), for only as long as required.
  • Encrypted backups, which are overwritten on a regular cycle and may hold your data for a short additional period (up to 30 days) before being permanently removed.

Important

  • Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. If you want to keep any of your content, save it before sending your request.
  • After deletion, you can create a new account at any time, but your previous data cannot be restored.
  • Uninstalling the app does not delete your account. Please send a request as described above.

Want to delete only some data?

If you want to remove specific uploaded documents or practice sets without deleting your account, email us at support@newtowns.in and tell us which items to remove.

For more on how we handle your data, see our Privacy Policy.
