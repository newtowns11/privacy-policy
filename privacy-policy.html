2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide to us

  • Account information: your name, email address, phone number and password when you sign up. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are stored in hashed (not readable) form; we cannot see your password.

2.2 Information generated through your use of the App

  • Test activity: your test attempts, including start and submission time, time spent, number of questions attempted, correct, incorrect and unanswered, marks obtained, percentage and completion status.
  • Processing status: the status of your PDF extraction jobs (for example processing, ready or failed) and any related error messages.
  • Search queries: terms you search for inside the App and your recent search history.

2.3 Information stored on your device

  • Your login session token and basic profile data are stored in your device's secure storage so you stay signed in. This data stays on your device and is removed when you sign out or uninstall the App.

4. How Your PDFs Are Processed

When you upload a PDF, it is stored in a private storage bucket linked to your account. To generate questions, our server creates a short-lived, time-limited secure link to the file, downloads it, and sends its contents to an AI model provider (see Section 5) which reads the document and returns structured questions and answers. The result is saved to your account so you can review and edit it before it becomes a practice set.

Please do not upload documents containing sensitive personal information (such as government ID numbers, bank details or medical records) as the content of the file is processed by the third-party services described below.
